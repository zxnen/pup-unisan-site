Students

The student portal lets you keep your basic profile and send questions to the campus. It shows only your own information.

Log in Create an account
Go to your dashboard

How the portal works

  1. Create an account with your email and a password, or continue with Google.
  2. Wait for approval. New accounts start as pending. A campus administrator reviews each one. While pending, you can only update your basic profile.
  3. Send inquiries once approved. You can follow the status of each inquiry and read the campus response in your dashboard.

Signing in with Google does not skip approval. Every account is reviewed the same way.
