Privacy Policy

This page describes what this website actually collects and why. Items marked [To be confirmed] are campus policies that have not been supplied yet; they will be filled in by the campus rather than guessed.

Browsing without an account

You can read public pages and send a campus inquiry without an account. For an inquiry, we collect your name, email address, optional message, category, a random first-party visitor identifier stored in this browser, and a one-way keyed hash of the connecting IP address for rate limiting and abuse review. If enabled, Cloudflare Turnstile checks the submission before it is accepted. If you use the homepage comment feature, we collect the display name and comment you submit along with the same visitor and abuse-prevention data.

Information collected when you register

  • Account details: your name, email address and password. Your password is handled by the authentication service (Supabase Auth) and is not stored in readable form by this website. If you use Google sign-in, we receive your name and email address from Google; we do not receive your Google password.
  • Account status: whether your account is pending, approved or rejected, and when an administrator reviewed it.
  • Optional profile details you choose to add: student number, program, year level, phone number, address and birthdate. You can leave these blank.
  • Account-linked inquiries: the subject, message, and optional phone number, program of interest and preferred contact method, plus any response from the campus and its status.
  • Public comments: your display name and message, moderation status, submission time, abuse signals, and any ban or review decisions associated with the visitor identifier.
  • Cookie choices: whether optional analytics are enabled. This version does not load an analytics service.

How the information is used

  • To let you log in and stay logged in. A session token is kept in your browser’s storage for this purpose.
  • To let campus administrators approve or reject student accounts.
  • To receive campus inquiries submitted by visitors and, for signed-in students, show account-linked inquiries and responses.
  • To publish or moderate comments and protect the form from abuse.
  • To remember and synchronize your cookie preferences.

Who can see it

  • Signed-in students can see their own profile and account-linked inquiries. Visitors cannot read inquiry records, and students cannot see other people’s information.
  • Campus administrators can see student profiles, inquiries, submitted comments and related moderation records to manage accounts, respond and review abuse reports.
  • Birthdates, addresses and phone numbers are never shown on public pages.
  • The faculty directory is public and shows each person’s name, position, department and the email address listed for them.

These restrictions are enforced in the database, not only in the interface.

Services this site relies on

The site is hosted on Netlify. Main website data and faculty directory data are stored in separate Supabase projects. Cloudflare Turnstile protects public comments. Some pages embed YouTube and Google Maps content; those providers may collect information according to their own policies when the embedded content loads. Google sign-in is provided by Google.
