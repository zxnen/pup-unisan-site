Welcome to PUP Unisan

Motto: "Tanglaw ng Bayan" (Light of the Nation)

The Polytechnic University of the Philippines – Unisan Campus has served the youth of Unisan, Quezon since 1987, offering accessible, quality higher education rooted in PUP's mission of national development.

Our History

PUP Unisan was established in 1987, originally conceived by local officials as a contribution to the welfare of out-of-school youth in the area. It began as a vocational-technical school before growing into a full satellite campus of the Polytechnic University of the Philippines, now offering undergraduate degree programs to students across the Unisan area and neighboring towns.

Vision

A Leading Comprehensive Polytechnic University in Asia.

Mission

Advance an Inclusive, Equitable, and Globally Relevant Polytechnic Education Towards National Development.
